I do not study at Högskolan Väst. Teknik and idrott are separate. I am the one who carries the answer from one room to the other. Biology I am walking in with is in `biology.md`. Correct that page if it is wrong.

How to ask: "I have a short biology note and three questions for this room. Which line is wrong?"

Status key: OPEN = unanswered. Do not fill these in from a blog.

## Idrott — salt or sugar

1. OPEN — For a person who has sweated, not for cholera: when is the WHO mix (Na 75, glucose 75, K 20 mmol/L) the wrong drink, and what replaces it?
2. OPEN — Is sweat sodium a usable proxy for "how much salt to put in the bottle," or does it only say what left the skin?
3. OPEN — What single number should change the mix: sweat Na, sweat rate, body-mass change, or none of these?
